About Swap Pro

Modern barter for people who want to trade, not spend

Our mission

Swap Pro brings barter into the digital age. We help people exchange goods and services without relying on fiat currency — connecting neighbors, reducing waste, and making fair trades easier to discover and complete.

How it works

List what you have, set your wishlist, and find matches nearby. When both parties agree on a swap, each pays a small prepaid transaction fee that is held until you meet at a verified Swap Pro hub. On-site officials help verify the exchange, and any value difference can be settled using your credit wallet.

Credits earned from uneven swaps stay in your wallet for future trades. They are designed to keep value flowing through the community — not to be withdrawn as cash.

Built on trust

No-shows hurt everyone. That is why Swap Pro uses prepaid commitments: if someone does not show up, they forfeit their fee. Combined with verified swap hubs, push notifications, and strike policies for repeat offenders, the platform is designed so both sides arrive ready to complete the trade.

What makes us different

  • Digital barter: Trade items and services directly with other users in your area.
  • Credit wallet: Fair value when one item is worth more than another.
  • Prepaid fees: Small commitments that reduce no-shows and build accountability.
  • Swap hubs: Safe, official meeting points with on-site verification.
  • Real-time updates: Notifications for requests, approvals, reminders, and outcomes.
