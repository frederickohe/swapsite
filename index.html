Digitalized barter trading system

Exchange goods and services without fiat currency. List what you have, set your wishlist, and discover matches in your area. Swap Pro brings the age-old practice of barter into a modern, trusted mobile experience.

Credit wallet for fair value

When your item is worth more than what you receive, the difference is added to your credit wallet. Use credits to settle future swaps — no cash needed. Credits are non-withdrawable and designed to keep trades fair and flowing.

Prepaid commitment, zero no-shows

Both parties pay a small transaction fee before a swap is confirmed. Fees are held by Swap Pro until you meet at a verified hub. No-shows forfeit their fee — building trust and ensuring everyone shows up ready to swap.

Safe swap hubs near you

Meet at official Swap Pro hubs with on-site verification. Get routed via Google Maps, settle any price differences on the spot, and complete your swap with confidence. Push notifications keep you informed every step of the way.
